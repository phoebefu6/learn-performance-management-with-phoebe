Learn Performance Management with Phoebe

Grow people - and part well when you must

Performance management is not the review form. It is the craft of ramping someone properly, coaching them every week, building a growth path they can see, and - rarely, and only after diagnosing why - running an improvement plan that is a genuine chance or ending things with dignity. This course teaches that craft end to end on an online retailer, Cartwheel: a leader track for CHROs and heads of function who design the system, and a manager track for team leads who run it. You will meet the four causes of underperformance and the four different interventions they need, the bias hiding inside the word "potential", and the two shortcuts that turn an improvement plan into a countdown. You also drive a live PIP Lab. For everyone else, it is a plain-English guide to what good management actually consists of.

An honest note on managing people: most underperformance is diagnosed wrong, and the wrong intervention costs months and sometimes a person. Skill needs teaching, will needs a real conversation, fit needs a different role, and context needs the context fixed - only one of those four is what an improvement plan is built for. This course teaches from the management canon (Grove, Project Oxygen, GROW, Buckingham and Goodall) and from published employment-practice guidance (Acas in the UK, the Tripartite Guidelines in Singapore). Cartwheel is a fictional teaching company and its people are invented. The PIP Lab is a deterministic teaching model, not a prediction engine. Regulations and codes are summarised for awareness and dated, not legal advice - employment law is local and fact-specific, so consult counsel before acting on a real case. And one figure you will not find here: the popular claim that "75% of people on a PIP leave anyway" traces to vendor blogs rather than research, so we do not repeat it.
Its sibling course: this one is deliberately the people half of a pair. Learn OKRs with Phoebe ↗ owns the measurement half - writing goals, designing fair metrics, rater bias and calibration mechanics, monitoring cadence, and diagnosing broken metrics - on the same fictional company, Cartwheel. Where this course needs a goal or a metric, it sends you there rather than repeating it.
